# Demo script — 10 minutes for management

**Before the meeting:** sign in as the owner. The sample business has 4 accounts — **General Motors** (Chevrolet, GMC, Cadillac), **Siddiqi**, **Ferrero** and **Agency internal** — with 3 teams and 10 people. You hold the Account Lead seat on the GM team, so My work and the bell already have items. To reload it with your own teammates' names on the GM team: Business settings → Accounts → Remove sample business, then **Load sample business** and type the names.

**0. The homepage (30 sec)** — user menu → *Home page*
What the platform is, in one scroll: accounts, access, tools, the brief workflow and the built-in help.

**1. One platform, every client (1 min)** — click the **account switcher** at the top left
"Like Ads Manager: every client account in one place." Open **All accounts**: open and overdue work, active briefs, briefs at risk, live campaigns and alerts for every account, side by side.

**2. Access by team (1.5 min)** — *Business settings*
*People*: everyone who can sign in, with their job role. *Teams*: the GM team edits General Motors; the Retail & FMCG team edits Siddiqi and Ferrero; Leadership can view every client account and change nothing. Give a team an account once, and everyone added to it later gets in too.

**3. Tools per account (1 min)** — switch to **Siddiqi** → gear → *Tools*
Team calendar and All tasks are off for this client, so they're gone from its sidebar. New accounts start with the essentials; *All tools* switches everything on. Switching off deletes nothing.

**4. A brief comes in (2 min)** — switch to **General Motors** → sidebar **New brief**
The form opens with a worked example. The hint under the dates shows the earliest realistic go-live from the workflow SLAs. **Submit** → pick the team → **Kick off**: each step gets an owner and a due date, and each person is notified.

**5. Nothing slips quietly (1.5 min)** — *Alerts*, then *Dashboard → Briefs & workflow*
Overdue and blocked steps, and briefs projected to miss go-live. **Bottlenecks by step** shows where the time goes — usually client approval.

**6. Help built in (1 min)** — press **?** (or *Guide & help* in the sidebar)
Ask "how do I give my team access?" — the answer comes with a button that opens the right screen. The Guide page has a getting-started checklist for each role.

**7. A viewer's view (1 min)** — Business settings → People → **View as** Hala Youssef (local mode only)
Leadership sees every client account and every number, with no buttons to change anything.

---

**After the demo:** Business settings → Accounts → **Remove sample business** (signed in as the owner).
