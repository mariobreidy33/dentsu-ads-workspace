# Demo script — 10 minutes for management

**Before the meeting:** sign in as the owner. The sample business loads by itself on a new platform: 7 fictional client accounts, 6 teams and 10 people. You hold the Account Lead seat in the Automotive team, so My work and the bell already have items. (If it was removed: Business settings → Accounts → Load sample business.)

**0. One platform, every client (1 min)** — click the **account switcher** at the top left
"Like Ads Manager: every client account in one place, each with its own logo." Search, then open **All accounts**: open and overdue work, active briefs, briefs at risk, live campaigns and alerts for every account, side by side. Sort by *Needs attention*.

**1. Access by team (1.5 min)** — *Business settings*
*People*: everyone who can sign in, with their job role and business role. *Teams*: the Automotive team edits Northwind Motors and Altavia Cars; Creative studio and Data & analytics work across most client accounts; Leadership can view every account and change nothing. Give a team an account once, and everyone added to it later gets in too.

**2. Tools per account (1 min)** — switch to **Harbor Bank** → gear → *Tools*
Calendar and Notebook are off for this client, so they're gone from its sidebar. Brightpath Academy doesn't use Brands. Switch a tool on, and it appears for everyone in that account. Switching off deletes nothing.

**3. A brief comes in (2 min)** — switch to **Northwind Motors** → sidebar **Brief**
The form opens with a worked example, so everyone can see what goes in each section. The hint under the dates shows the earliest realistic go-live from the workflow SLAs. **Submit** → pick the team → **Kick off**: each step gets an owner and a due date, and each person gets one email. The subject starts with the account name, and the link opens that account.

**4. Nothing slips quietly (1.5 min)** — *Alerts*, then *Dashboard → Briefs & workflow*
Overdue and blocked steps, and briefs projected to miss go-live. **Bottlenecks by step**: *Client approval of plan* runs at about 2× its SLA and *Creative production* at about 1.5×. That's the conversation to have with clients.

**5. A viewer's view (1 min)** — Business settings → People → **View as** Hala Youssef (local mode only)
Leadership sees every account and every number, with no buttons to change anything. Switch back with *View as* on your own row. (Online, each person simply signs in with their own email.)

**6. The audit trail (1 min)** — *Activity log*
Every action with who and when, per account. Entries can't be edited or deleted. Export to Excel.

---

**After the demo:** Business settings → Accounts → **Remove sample business** (signed in as the owner).
